# Nova Researcher v1

## Goal

Researcher v1 gives Nova a structured research capability for discovering and investigating potentially exploitable market mechanisms. It is not a claim that Nova can already find a real edge.

## Roles

### Nova

Nova is the discovery system. She should:

1. identify a research question;
2. propose a plausible mechanism;
3. formulate a falsifiable hypothesis;
4. design a primary test;
5. identify legitimate development-only exploration;
6. record risks and expected failure modes;
7. learn from failed formulations without silently rewriting the evidence;
8. produce a research brief for assessment.

### External assessor (current stage)

ChatGPT is the independent assessor. It checks methodology, evidence quality, leakage, search burden, costs, causal interpretation, failure diagnosis, and whether a candidate deserves confirmation.

### Future autonomous assessor

Nova may eventually perform its own assessment, confirmation, and replication. That capability must be demonstrated against unseen research problems before ChatGPT is removed as the external check.

## Research constitution

- A plausible mechanism comes before parameter search.
- Development and confirmation are separate.
- Confirmation data is never used to choose a formulation.
- Exploration can be iterative, including aggressive tuning, but the entire search history is recorded and the resulting candidate is treated as discovery rather than proof.
- A failed formulation does not automatically kill the underlying mechanism.
- Repeated optimization increases skepticism; it never increases the amount of independent evidence.
- Research gates, costs, and success definitions are not changed merely to rescue a result.
- "No edge found" is a valid research outcome.
- A backtest that looks profitable is only a candidate until fresh confirmation and replication support it.
- The long-term trader must be adaptive and allowed to stay out when the current market does not match a useful learned condition.

## First milestone

Do not optimize trading performance yet. First prove that Nova can consistently produce better-structured, more falsifiable, less repetitive research proposals than the current research generator while preserving the deterministic experiment and evidence boundaries.
