# Nova Research Capability Scorecard v1

The scorecard evaluates research judgment, not profitability.

## Scoring

Each dimension is scored 0-4.

- 0 = absent or materially wrong
- 1 = weak / mostly superficial
- 2 = partially correct but important gaps
- 3 = strong and defensible
- 4 = excellent, specific, and appropriately skeptical

Total: 48 points across 12 dimensions.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | What the assessor asks |
|---|---|
| Problem understanding | Did Nova identify the actual research question and distinguish discovery from claiming an edge? |
| Assumption challenge | Did Nova challenge the premise, identify confounders, and consider reasons the relationship may not exist? |
| Mechanism diversity | Did Nova consider genuinely different causal/mechanical explanations rather than indicator variants? |
| Experiment discrimination | Does the proposed experiment distinguish competing explanations rather than merely measure profitability? |
| Falsification | Are failure conditions explicit, predeclared, and capable of killing the hypothesis? |
| Causal reasoning | Does the proposed evidence support the claimed mechanism, rather than merely correlation or backtest performance? |
| Anti-overfitting | Does Nova control search degrees of freedom, multiple testing, leakage, and confirmation contamination? |
| Uncertainty | Does Nova account for sample size, variance, confidence intervals, and uncertainty in conclusions? |
| Economic realism | Does the design account for costs, execution assumptions, and whether an effect survives economically? |
| Failure learning | Does prior evidence actually change the proposed research path? |
| Information value | Does Nova choose experiments based on how much uncertainty they can remove per unit cost? |
| Stopping discipline | Can Nova explicitly reject weak paths or stop when additional testing is unlikely to be informative? |

## Critical-failure gates

A high total score cannot rescue any of these failures:

1. Uses confirmation data for discovery.
2. Changes a preregistered gate after seeing results.
3. Claims an edge from development performance alone.
4. Repackages the same mechanism as multiple independent hypotheses.
5. Treats implementation failure as proof that the mechanism is false without checking the distinction.
6. Ignores material transaction costs or execution assumptions.
7. Continues parameter shopping after a failed family without a new mechanism.

A critical failure marks the run as **RESEARCH-INVALID**, regardless of numerical score.

## Interpretation bands

- 40-48: strong research behavior; suitable for broader autonomous exploration with continued external assessment
- 32-39: promising but material weaknesses remain
- 24-31: useful structured assistant, not yet reliable autonomous researcher
- below 24: strategy generator / weak research judgment

The numerical score is a development metric. It is not proof that Nova can find a trading edge.
