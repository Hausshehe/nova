# Nova Research Capability Baseline

## Purpose

Measure whether Nova can make useful research decisions before experimental outcomes are available. This is a capability benchmark, not a profitability benchmark.

## Rule

Nova receives only the scenario, constraints, and prior evidence supplied below. It must decide what to investigate next before seeing any new experiment result.

## Scenario

Investigate whether short-horizon predictability in XAGUSD 4H returns is genuinely conditional on market context, or whether apparent context effects are likely to be artifacts of threshold selection, regime definition, or short-lived sample structure.

Nova must not assume momentum, mean reversion, volatility, RSI, moving averages, or any named indicator family in advance.

## Prior evidence

Two broad development avenues have already failed to establish a robust unconditional short-horizon edge: continuation and mean-reversion formulations. These failures do not prove that context-conditioned effects are impossible, but they should affect research priority and experimental design.

## Constraints

- No confirmation-data inspection.
- No post-result parameter rescue.
- Development exploration must be bounded and accounted for as a research family.
- Costs and sample-size limitations must be considered before testing.
- A valid outcome is rejection or stopping.
- Nova must distinguish implementation failure from mechanism failure.

## Required decision

Before running anything, Nova must produce:

1. Its interpretation of the actual research question.
2. At least two genuinely competing mechanisms or explanations.
3. Which weak explanations it rejects and why.
4. The highest-information experiment available within the budget.
5. The prediction that would distinguish the competing explanations.
6. A preregistered falsifier.
7. The main overfitting, leakage, and multiple-testing risks.
8. What evidence would cause it to update toward or away from each mechanism.
9. A stopping rule.
10. The exact next action.

## Assessment

ChatGPT remains the external assessor. Agreement with ChatGPT is not the scoring criterion. The criterion is whether Nova's decision is logically justified, discriminative, statistically disciplined, and useful for narrowing the research space.
