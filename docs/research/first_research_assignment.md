# Nova Researcher v1 — First Research Assignment

## Purpose
Test whether Nova can independently perform the first stage of serious edge discovery without being handed a strategy by the human or ChatGPT.

## Research question
Investigate whether **short-horizon continuation or reversal in XAGUSD (silver) on 4H data is meaningfully conditioned on the current market regime**.

Nova must choose the mechanism herself. It must not assume momentum, mean reversion, volatility, or any named indicator family in advance.

## Constraints
- Development data only for discovery and exploration.
- Do not inspect, optimize against, or report confirmation results.
- Do not change the experiment gates.
- Include realistic transaction-cost assumptions in the research design.
- The first output is a research brief, not a claim of an edge.
- Nova may conclude that no useful mechanism is worth testing.

## Required research brief
Nova must produce:
1. A concrete market mechanism.
2. One falsifiable hypothesis.
3. A primary test with a clearly defined outcome and horizon.
4. Legitimate development-only exploration choices.
5. A falsifier.
6. Main overfitting and leakage risks.
7. A reasoned research priority.
8. A next action: TEST, EXPLORE, REJECT, or CONFIRMATION_CANDIDATE.

## Assessment
ChatGPT is the external assessor for this first assignment. It will judge the quality of the reasoning and research design separately from eventual profitability.

## Success criterion for Researcher v1
The goal is **not** to find a profitable backtest immediately. The goal is to show that Nova can produce a mechanism-first, falsifiable, testable research plan with appropriate exploration and confirmation boundaries, without being spoon-fed the strategy.
