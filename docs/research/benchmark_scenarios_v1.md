# Benchmark Scenarios v1

These scenarios are intentionally different. Nova must not infer that the answer is always momentum, reversal, or regime conditioning.

## Scenario A — XAGUSD short-horizon context

**Question:** Determine whether a repeatable short-horizon directional relationship exists in XAGUSD 4H data and whether it is conditional on observable context.

**Trap:** The recent research history contains failed continuation and mean-reversion formulations. Nova must use those failures without assuming that the entire underlying search space is exhausted.

**What this tests:** failure learning, mechanism diversity, experiment selection, stopping.

## Scenario B — Synthetic hidden mechanism

**Question:** Given a development dataset generated by an unknown process, determine which of several plausible causal mechanisms best explains a target variable and design the minimum experiment needed to discriminate them.

**Trap:** One candidate has the strongest superficial correlation but is not the data-generating mechanism.

**What this tests:** causal reasoning, confounding awareness, discrimination, resistance to correlation chasing.

## Scenario C — Indicator-shopping trap

**Question:** A weak development result appears after testing several indicators. Decide whether another indicator family should be tested.

**Trap:** The proposed next indicator is highly correlated with already-tested variables and therefore provides little new information.

**What this tests:** search-space reasoning, information value, multiple-testing awareness, ability to reject an attractive but redundant experiment.

## Scenario D — Failure interpretation

**Question:** A promising mechanism fails one formulation but has not yet been tested under the mechanism's stated boundary conditions.

**Trap:** Either immediately kill the mechanism or immediately tune parameters.

**What this tests:** distinction between mechanism failure and implementation failure, plus disciplined follow-up design.

## Scenario E — Stop decision

**Question:** Several genuinely distinct tests have failed after costs, across relevant regimes, with no coherent residual mechanism.

**Trap:** Continue searching because the project has not found an edge yet.

**What this tests:** stopping discipline and opportunity-cost awareness.

## Scoring rule

Nova must provide its decision before outcome information is exposed. The assessor then scores the decision using the capability benchmark and separately records whether the eventual empirical conclusion was correct.
