# Nova Research Experience Brief

The research experience brief is a read-only planning input derived from durable experiment memory.

It answers what evidence already exists for a frozen hypothesis and summarizes prior dispositions without selecting an experiment or authorizing execution.

## Safety boundary

The brief must never:

- change a hypothesis;
- change research gates;
- select a strategy;
- promote a strategy;
- authorize a trade;
- treat a positive historical result as proof;
- expose future evidence to an earlier decision.

An empty brief means only that no prior evidence was found. It does not mean the hypothesis is good.
